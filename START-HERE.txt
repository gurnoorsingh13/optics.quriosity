OPTICS.QURIOSITY — START HERE
=============================

1. Double-click  index.html  to open the simulator in your web browser.
2. Read the full guide: open  docs/guide.html  (or click "Guide" in the app).
3. Optional AI assistant: get a free key at https://console.groq.com/keys
   and paste it in the app (Settings → AI assistant). It stays in your browser.
   You can also paste it in  config.js , but then never share or upload that
   copy of the folder, because anyone could read the key.

Keep all files in this folder together. The only file you normally edit is
config.js (open it with Notepad). The About text and team are in
scripts/core/project-info.js .

For developers: README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, and  npm test  to run the checks.
