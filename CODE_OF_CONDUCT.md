# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who is curious about quantum optics, whatever their background, level of experience, nationality, gender, identity, age, disability, religion or appearance.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to physics or to programming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Credit the work of others, including the authors of papers, data and drawings you use.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email the maintainers privately at **contact@quriositygroup.in** (or **quriositygroup@gmail.com**). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do so.
